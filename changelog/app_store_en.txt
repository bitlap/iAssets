iAssets — Clear Overview, Easy Tracking

Keep stocks, crypto, savings and other investments together. See how your assets are allocated and how your returns change over time.

Stocks and Dividends
• Search by name or symbol across US, Hong Kong, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Beijing, Japanese, Korean, UK and over-the-counter markets
• Filter search results, holdings and dividends by individual exchange or market, with clear color labels
• Recognize your holdings more easily with company logos across more markets
• Record buys, sells, dividends and fees, with automatic calculations for net invested, profit and loss and after-tax dividends
• Stock assets reflect current holdings only; net sale proceeds remain a separate transaction statistic
• Fully selling a position removes the stock and related records; existing closed-position history is not deleted automatically
• See assets, total profit and loss and today's profit and loss at a glance; expand a holding for trade details and actions
• Add custom stocks with a currency picker beside the price and an easy-to-reach button below
• Quotes from Eastmoney, Tencent and Yahoo Finance; some market quotes may be delayed
• Stock search shows prices and currency codes, with more display currencies supported

Crypto Assets
• Connect regular Binance accounts or lead trader accounts to view spot balances, futures positions and estimated asset values
• Refresh automatically or pull to refresh, and include crypto in your total assets and allocation
• Viewing only, with no trading or transfers; copy trader portfolios are not currently supported

Asset Overview
• Organize cash, deposits and wealth products with colorful cards; regional templates offer 401(k) / IRA, MPF or provident fund, with Property and Other Assets in the common templates
• Create custom asset categories and valuation methods, rename categories, and move assets between them
• See deposit rates and time remaining, or wealth-product shares and NAV, beside balances
• See creation and update times clearly, with collapsible groups and drag-to-reorder
• View stocks, crypto and other assets together, with support for multiple display currencies
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show total assets, category values and update time; eligible background refreshes sync crypto and update widgets when iOS permits
• See FX rate status and data dates; missing rates mark totals incomplete while original-currency assets remain visible

Made for Everyday Use
• Optionally lock the app with Face ID or Touch ID when opening or returning; use your device passcode to regain access
• Hide amounts in assets, holdings and earnings with one tap; a privacy cover protects the app preview when switching away
• Sync stocks, manually managed assets and settings with iCloud; view and record stocks and manual assets offline
• Crypto connection details stay on your device; crypto balances and positions are not synced to iCloud
• Choose Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese or English, light or dark themes, and your preferred gain/loss colors
• Other system languages default to English; gain/loss colors start with regional defaults and remain customizable
• No ads or social feed; basic features are free, with subscription and lifetime Pro options
